# Electrical & Streetlight Infrastructure Guidelines

## 1. Overview
Covers broken streetlights, snapping electrical wires, transformer sparks, low-voltage issues, and damaged poles.

## 2. Safety Risk Assessment
- **Critical (Snapping Wires / Live Conductors)**: Fallen live electrical wires or sparking low-hanging conductors. IMMEDIATE HUMAN ESCALATION & KSEB LINE ISOLATION REQUIRED.
- **High**: Multiple broken streetlights on dangerous unlit road junctions or school zones causing safety hazards at night.
- **Medium**: Single broken streetlight in residential lane.
- **Low**: Minor flickering bulb report.

## 3. Department Routing
- Primary Department: **State Electricity Board (KSEB) / Panchayat Electrical Maintenance**
