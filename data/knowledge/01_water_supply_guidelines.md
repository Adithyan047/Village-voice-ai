# Rural Water Supply Complaint Guidelines

## 1. Overview & Scope
This guideline applies to public drinking water infrastructure in rural panchayats and wards. It covers pipe leakages, motor failures, pump house disruptions, total water supply interruptions, and water contamination.

## 2. Severity Classification Matrix
- **Critical (Duration > 48 hours OR Contamination Risk)**: Complete absence of drinking water for an entire locality/ward exceeding 48 hours, or reported chemical/biological contamination causing public health hazards. Requires immediate dispatch of tanker supplies within 6 hours.
- **High (Duration 24–48 hours)**: Intermittent or zero supply affecting over 10 households for more than 24 hours.
- **Medium (Duration 12–24 hours)**: Low pressure or localized pipe leakages affecting 1–10 households.
- **Low (Duration < 12 hours)**: Minor maintenance issues, scheduled maintenance notifications, or minor tap repairs.

## 3. Mandatory Department Routing
- Primary Department: **Public Health Engineering Department (PHED) / Kerala Water Authority (KWA)**
- Escalation Contact: **Gram Panchayat Executive Officer / Water Committee Chairman**

## 4. Emergency Action Standard Operating Procedure
In cases where school, clinic, or dense residential area has no water for > 48 hours, dispatch local emergency tanker supply immediately while main pipe repair is underway.
