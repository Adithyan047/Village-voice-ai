# Comprehensive Civic Issue Severity Matrix

| Category | Low Severity (1-39) | Medium Severity (40-69) | High Severity (70-84) | Critical Severity (85-100) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Water Supply** | Low pressure < 6 hrs | Intermittent supply 12-24 hrs | No water 24-48 hrs | No water > 48 hrs or Contaminated water |
| **Roads** | Loose gravel / minor surface degradation | Small potholes on village lane | Deep potholes on main bus route | Road collapse / culvert break / landslide |
| **Waste Management** | Minor roadside litter | Trash bin unemptied 2-3 days | Bin overflow 4-6 days | Rotting waste > 7 days or dumping in well |
| **Sanitation / Drain** | Minor drain odor | Partial silt blockage | Open drain flooding lane | Live raw sewage spilling on public road |
| **Electricity** | Single light flicker | Single unlit pole | Entire dark junction near school | Fallen live wire / sparking transformer |
