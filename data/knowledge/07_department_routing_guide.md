# Department Routing & Governance Matrix

## Department Mapping Rules
1. **Water Supply** -> Kerala Water Authority (KWA) / PHED Wing
2. **Roads** -> Public Works Department (PWD) Roads Division / Panchayat Engineering
3. **Waste Management** -> Haritha Karma Sena / Panchayat Health Inspector
4. **Sanitation & Drainage** -> Gram Panchayat Sanitation Taskforce
5. **Electricity & Streetlights** -> KSEB Electrical Section / Panchayat Lighting Cell
6. **Public Safety** -> Village Executive Officer & Local Police Station Liaison

## Escalation Triggers
- Priority Score > 85 automatically triggers SMS / Email notification to Ward Member & Department Head.
- Confidence Score < 0.60 routes issue directly to **Human Escalation Queue** on Authority Dashboard.
