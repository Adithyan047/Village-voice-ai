# Drainage & Sanitation Infrastructure Guidelines

## 1. Overview
Covers open drain blockages, sewage overflow, public toilet maintenance, and monsoon drain clogging.

## 2. Severity Classification
- **Critical**: Sewage overflow spilling into public residential walkways or near schools; heavy drain blockage causing street flooding during rain.
- **High**: Stagnant drain water breeding mosquitoes for > 5 days.
- **Medium**: Partial drain obstruction due to silt/debris buildup.
- **Low**: Odor complaint without visible sewage overflow.

## 3. Department Routing
- Primary Department: **Panchayat Sanitation Wing / Health Inspection Team**
