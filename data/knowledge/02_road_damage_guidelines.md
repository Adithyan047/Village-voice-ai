# Road Damage & Transportation Infrastructure Guidelines

## 1. Overview
Covers rural village roads, panchayat connecting roads, culverts, small bridges, and school pedestrian paths.

## 2. Hazard Severity Criteria
- **Critical / High Safety Risk**: Large deep potholes on main school routes, caving roads, collapsed culverts, or landslides blocking village access roads. Potholes > 15cm depth near school zones mandate immediate warning sign placement and emergency patching within 24 hours.
- **High**: Deep rutting, extensive surface erosion affecting public transport / bus routes.
- **Medium**: Minor potholes, degraded side shoulders on interior village lanes.
- **Low**: Faded street signs, minor gravel loose sections.

## 3. Department Routing
- Primary Department: **Public Works Department (PWD) / Panchayat Engineering Wing**
