# Waste Management & Garbage Accumulation Guidelines

## 1. Overview
Covers rural solid waste management, community garbage dump sites, overflowing public waste bins, organic waste rotting, and hazardous dumping near water bodies.

## 2. Priority & Risk Rules
- **Critical (Uncollected Waste > 7 days OR Near Water Body)**: Rotting uncollected waste lying for over 7 days, or illegal dumping near public drinking wells/streams. High risk of disease vectors (dengue, cholera).
- **High**: Overflowing public market bins uncollected for > 3 days.
- **Medium**: Plastic waste accumulation on roadside.
- **Low**: Isolated minor littering complaints.

## 3. Department Routing
- Primary Department: **Haritha Karma Sena / Panchayat Health & Sanitation Department**
